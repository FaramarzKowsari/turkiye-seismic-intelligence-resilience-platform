# Public-sector and research use cases

- Transparent regional seismicity dashboards
- Post-event catalogue exploration
- Aftershock-sequence descriptive analysis
- Municipal facility exposure screening with authorised local inventories
- Research reproducibility and student training
- Sentinel-1 acquisition planning for deformation studies
- Data-quality audits across catalogue providers
- Open-science release pipelines with DOI and citation metadata

The platform is not an operational command-and-control or emergency-warning product.
