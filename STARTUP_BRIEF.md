# Startup brief

## Problem

Earthquake catalogues, satellite acquisitions, exposure inventories and analytical tools are often
fragmented. This slows transparent research and early-stage public-sector prototyping.

## Product

DepremNabız AI combines catalogue ingestion, quality control, analytics, satellite-product
discovery, exposure screening and reproducible reporting in a single open platform.

## Initial users

Universities, municipal data teams, resilience researchers, civil-society analysts, insurers,
consultancies and geospatial software developers.

## Free/open operating model

The core runs on public APIs, user-provided data, GitHub, Streamlit Community Cloud and Zenodo.
Commercial services can later focus on deployment, integration, validated local datasets and
expert-reviewed reports rather than locking the core software.
