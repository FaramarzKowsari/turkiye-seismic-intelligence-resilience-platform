# Data card

## Demo earthquake catalogue

- Synthetic and deterministic
- Designed to exercise data validation, charting, clustering and export code
- Not suitable for hazard, policy or operational conclusions

## Demo exposure points

- Synthetic population/facility points
- Used only to demonstrate radius-based exposure calculations
- Not a representation of actual residents or infrastructure

## Live USGS catalogue

- Queried through the official FDSN Event Web Service
- Bounding box defaults to Türkiye and nearby offshore regions
- Event attributes may be revised by the provider

## Satellite catalogue

- Public Sentinel-1 GRD metadata discovered through the Copernicus Data Space STAC service
- Catalogue discovery is not the same as InSAR displacement processing
